AI-Powered Interview System

Enhanced the interview system with AI-powered transcription, summaries, and assisted feedback writing to reduce manual effort and improve interview efficiency.

RoleUI/UX Design
ResultsiOS & Android
DateFrom 2024

CIS (Candidate Interview System) is an internal interview platform that enables interviewers to conduct video interviews and complete structured evaluations during the hiring process.

Problems in the Interview Evaluation Flow

Heavy reliance on memory and manual notes

Evaluation often happened hours or days after the interview, making it easy to forget important details and introduce inaccuracies.

Inconsistent feedback quality

Interviewers varied widely in how they structured and detailed their evaluations, making candidates difficult to compare objectively.

Low completion rates

Completing a scorecard required additional time to recall and organize the conversation, which could lead to delays or skipped evaluations.

Stakeholder needs

Recruiters need high completion rates, timely feedback, consistent evaluation structures, and sufficient evidence to move candidates through the hiring process.

Interviewers want to save time without feeling that AI is making the decision for them. Their priority is simple: make the evaluation fast to complete while keeping judgment and accountability in human hands.

Goal

The goal was to use AI to transcribe and summarize interviews, then provide suggestions for completing the scorecard. The experience needed to improve evaluation speed and quality while preserving the interviewer's ability to make an independent hiring decision.

Feature architecture: Adapt interactions to users’ reading goals

Full-page view provide a comfortable reading experience

When interviewers want to review the interview as a whole, they are in an exploratory reading mode. They need a full page with a comfortable reading width—not a form that compresses the content.

Linked drawer support the review-and-verify workflow

When completing a scorecard, interviewers use the scorecard and AI suggestions as their main workspace, with the transcript and AI summary as supporting evidence. References open in a push drawer beside the scorecard, so interviewers can compare and verify evidence without leaving the page.
